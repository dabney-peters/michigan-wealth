Our Teams

Five teams keep MWAG running.

Each Vice President leads a small team of Associates. Here's what each team actually does.

Social Affairs Associate

Community, mentorship, service, and the weekly newsletter. Reports to the VP of Social Affairs
What the team doesBuilds the club's community: networking mixers, casual events that bring members together, mentorship, philanthropy and service, and the weekly "This week at MWAG" newsletter. The team also plans events on dates with no guest speaker.
Good fit ifYou enjoy bringing people together and following through on the details.

Marketing Associate

Events, social media, flyers, and member growth. Reports to the VP of Marketing
What the team doesMakes MWAG visible and keeps its membership growing: Diag boards, Fall and Winter Festival marketing, social media accounts, and the photos and flyers behind every event.
Good fit ifYou create content or design, and you care how the club looks and sounds.

Operations Associate

Rooms, attendance, speakers, and outside contact. Reports to the VP of Operations
What the team doesKeeps the club running day to day: room bookings, attendance, internal coordination, and contact with speakers and anyone outside the club. The team also brings forward guest speaker and business partnership opportunities.
Good fit ifYou are organized and reliable, and comfortable writing to people outside the club.

Finance Associate

Weekly professional development and networking. Reports to the VP of Finance
What the team doesBuilds members' professional readiness: weekly development opportunities between Friday events, including professional headshots, an investments workshop, resume review, and interview prep, plus introductions to people working in the field.
Good fit ifYou care about career readiness and like connecting people with the right contact.

Recruitment Associate

Applications, dues, member data, and the festivals. Reports to the VP of Recruitment
What the team doesOwns how members and Associates join: the application process, membership dues, member data, and leading the Fall and Winter Festival, where MWAG meets prospective members.
Good fit ifYou are discreet and fair, comfortable with spreadsheets, and glad to talk with prospective members.
