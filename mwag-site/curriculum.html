The Program

A financial life, mapped.

Eight sessions, in order, from kickoff to estate planning. Click a date to see what we cover.

Sept 15 · 6:30–8:30 PM

Kickoff & Info Session

An introduction to MWAG: what the semester covers, how meetings run, and how to become an active member. No background required. Just show up. (location pending)

Sept 22 · Week 2

Cash Flow & Budgeting

The foundation every other topic builds on: how to structure income, spending, and savings before anything more complex enters the picture.

Sept 29 · Week 3

Risk Management & Insurance

Life, disability, and umbrella coverage; The planning work that protects everything else you build.

Oct 6 · Week 4

Investments

Asset allocation and diversification, taught through a goals-based planning lens and how an advisor thinks about a portfolio.

Oct 13 · Week 5

Retirement Planning

401(k)s, 403(b)s, IRAs, and Social Security — how a plan gets built for the decades after a paycheck stops.

Oct 27 · Week 6

Compensation & Benefits

Equity compensation, executive benefits, and how to read and negotiate a total comp package, drawn directly from Executive Wealth practice at Goldman Sachs Ayco.

Nov 3 · Week 7

Tax Planning

Tax-advantaged accounts and the basics of income and capital gains planning. This is the thread that runs through every other topic.

Nov 10 · Week 8

Estate Planning

Wills, trusts, and beneficiary planning. This is the closing topic of the semester, and the one that ties every prior session together.
